LockTab Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

LockTab is a Chrome extension by GreedHunter Academy that password-protects your Chrome profile, windows and tabs. This policy explains what information LockTab handles and what it does with it.

Summary

LockTab does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. It has no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no user accounts. Everything it stores stays on your device.

What LockTab stores on your device

LockTab uses Chrome's extension storage (chrome.storage.local and chrome.storage.session) on your computer to keep:

  • A password verifier, not your password. When you create a master password, LockTab derives a key from it (PBKDF2-SHA-256, 210,000 iterations, random salt) and stores a small value encrypted with that key (AES-256-GCM). Your password and recovery key are never stored.
  • The display name you entered (optional), shown on the lock screen.
  • Your settings, such as startup lock, auto-lock time and strict mode.
  • Protection state: random identifiers for the tabs and windows you chose to protect, and whether they are currently locked. For locked tabs showing the full-page lock screen, LockTab temporarily keeps the page address so it can take you back after you unlock.
  • Failed-attempt counters used for brute-force protection.

For tabs you protect, LockTab also saves a random identifier in that page's sessionStorage so protection can be restored when Chrome reopens the tab. The identifier contains no personal information.

What LockTab does not do

  • It does not read, collect or change the content of the web pages you visit.
  • It does not track your browsing history.
  • It does not send any data over the network. LockTab makes no network requests.
  • It does not use cookies, analytics or third-party code.

Permissions and why they are needed

PermissionWhy
Access to all sites (<all_urls>), scripting To show the lock screen on top of any page you choose to lock.
tabs To know which tab or window to lock and to restore pages after unlocking.
storage To keep the password verifier, settings and protection state on your device.
idle To auto-lock after inactivity or when your computer locks, if you turn this on.

Removing your data

Remove LockTab from chrome://extensions and Chrome deletes all of LockTab's stored data. Page sessionStorage identifiers are discarded when the tab closes.

Children

LockTab is a general-purpose utility and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published with the extension's store listing with an updated date.

Contact

Questions: contact the developer through the support link on LockTab's Chrome Web Store listing, or through greedhunteracademy.com.
